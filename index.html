Late November Guide

Nov 24 Kualoa Ranch UTV Check-In (2:15 PM)

Kualoa Ranch strictly requires arrival 45 minutes prior (by 2:15 PM) for your 3:00 PM UTV Raptor Tour for waivers, helmets, and safety orientation. Closed-toe shoes are mandatory, and expect red Ka'a'awa Valley dust!

Hanauma Bay 48-Hr Reservation Rule

For your Nov 24 morning snorkel, book online via Honolulu Parks & Recreation on Nov 22 at 7:00 AM HST sharp (tickets sell out in under 5 minutes). Note: Tuesday Nov 24 is open!

North Shore Winter Swell Advisory

Late November brings massive North Pacific winter swells. Skip snorkeling at Shark's Cove or Three Tables (watch surfers there instead!) and focus snorkeling on protected South (Hanauma Bay) and Leeward (Electric Beach) shores.

Hanauma Bay 48-Hr Reservation Rule

Tickets release online via Honolulu Parks & Recreation exactly 48 hours in advance at 7:00 AM HST and sell out in under 5 minutes. Closed Mondays & Tuesdays.

Rental Car & Parking Strategy

Keeping your rental strictly to Days 2 & 3 avoids $45–$55/night Waikiki resort valet fees on Nights 1 & 3, while giving you full freedom for West/North and Windward loops.
